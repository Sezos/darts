Delete your DartScore account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open DartScore and go to Settings.
  2. Under Online account, tap Delete online account and confirm.

This deletes your profile, friend code, friend connections, game backups and online account. Games saved on your phone stay until you delete the app.

By email

If you can't open the app, email badral@mail.com with your friend code (shown under Friends, for example 7K2-QXP) or the Apple/Google email you signed in with. We delete the same data within 30 days.
