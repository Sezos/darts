DartScore Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Without going online

Scores, games, players and settings stay on your phone. Nothing about your games leaves the phone until you open Friends.

Analytics and ads

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) records how the app is used: screens opened, games started and finished, settings changed, app language, and basic device information. We use this to improve the app. It isn't tied to your name.
  • Google AdMob shows ads and may collect device identifiers, approximate location from your IP address, and ad interactions. See how Google uses this data.

Friends and ranks (only if you open Friends)

  • The app creates an anonymous online account and a profile with your player name, a friend code and your skill ratings (3-dart average, marks per round, games played). Anyone who has your code or QR can see this profile.
  • Your friends list is private to you.
  • A private backup of your game history lets your ranks survive a reinstall or a new phone. Only you can read it.
  • If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, Firebase Authentication stores the account identifier and email address that provider shares. Other players never see it.
  • The camera is used only to scan friends' QR codes, on the phone. No images are stored or sent.

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore in Hong Kong, region asia-east2).

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Online account → Delete online account. This removes your profile, friend code, friend connections (on both sides), game backups and online account straight away. Games on your phone stay until you delete the app.

Can't open the app? See how to request deletion.
